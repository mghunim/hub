HUB Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

HUB is a community app for compound residents and their management. This policy explains what information HUB collects, why, and how you can remove it.

Information we collect

  • From your Google account: your name and email address, used only to sign you in. We never see your Google password.
  • Profile details you enter: mobile number, whether you are the unit's owner, tenant or family member, and optionally the last 4 digits of your national ID.
  • Unit details: district, community, unit number and unit type.
  • Activity in the app: requests, service bookings, complaints, gate passes, gate notifications, violations and notifications.

How we use it

  • To verify that you live in or own a unit in the compound.
  • To let the compound management handle your requests, complaints, gate passes and announcements.
  • To send you in-app notifications about your unit and requests.

We do not sell your information, show ads, or share it with anyone other than your compound's management.

Who can see your information

You can see your own information. The app manager of your compound can see your profile, units, requests and complaints in order to manage them. Other residents cannot see your information.

Where it is stored

Data is stored securely with Supabase (servers in Frankfurt, Germany), with access rules that limit each person to their own records.

Deleting your data

Open the side menu, tap your name, then Delete my account. This permanently removes your profile, units, passes, requests and complaints. You can also ask the app manager to delete them.
